Your league, plus the intelligence Sleeper doesn't show you

LeagueLens is a companion to Sleeper, not a replacement. Sleeper stays where you manage lineups, waivers, and scoring — LeagueLens pools player information from Sleeper, KeepTradeCut, FantasyCalc, FantasyPros, and more into one place, so you're not tabbing between sites. The flagship view is the unified Player Profile: one canonical page per player, compiled from everywhere.
